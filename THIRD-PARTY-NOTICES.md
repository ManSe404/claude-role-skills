No code or text is copied. The references of the ai-architect skills are written anew; they credit
the ideas they build on: Anthropic's *Building effective agents* (the workflow and agent shapes),

the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and Simon Willison's *lethal trifecta* for the review list.
