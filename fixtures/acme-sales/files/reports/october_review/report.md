# October sales review (draft for the Friday meeting)

Prepared by Milena Hrast on 5 November 2026 for the leadership team. Numbers come from the queries in this folder;
their output is saved under `output/`. Data was refreshed on 5 November.

## Headlines

1. Net revenue in October was EUR 88600.00, led by the standard line (`revenue_by_line.sql`).
2. By region, the three regions together brought EUR 81842.62 (`revenue_by_region.sql`).
3. October is up 14.5% on September, EUR 85390.00 against EUR 74607.00 (`sep_vs_oct.sql`).
4. Web conversion was 5.22% in October, against a target of 4% (`conversion.sql`).
5. Desktop converts at 4.43% and mobile at 1.81%, so we propose moving mobile budget to desktop
   (`conversion_by_device.sql`).
6. The average order in a store was EUR 118.89. Store 6 is our best store with an average order of
   EUR 240.00, and we propose it as the model for the others (`avg_order_by_store.sql`).
