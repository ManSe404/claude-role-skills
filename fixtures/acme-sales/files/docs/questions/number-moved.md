# Why did net revenue fall in October?

The Monday run of the dashboard (2 November, 06:00 UTC) showed net revenue of EUR 81,190.00 for
October against EUR 96,000.00 for September, a drop of EUR 14,810.00. Finance net revenue is
`net_amount` of completed orders by order date. The leadership team wants to know why it fell. Sample
data is in `data/orders.csv`; `loaded_at` is when each row reached the warehouse.
