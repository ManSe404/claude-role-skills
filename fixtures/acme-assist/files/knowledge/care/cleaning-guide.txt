CLEANING AND DESCALING GUIDE
Kestrel K-200, Kestrel K-300, MF-1 milk frother

EVERY DAY
- Empty and rinse the drip tray (E4 when it is full).
- K-300: empty the grounds container.
- K-200: rinse the portafilter and the baskets; wipe the steam wand after each use.
- MF-1: wash the jug with a soft sponge after each use.

EVERY WEEK
- K-200: brush the group head and the shower screen.
- K-300: take out the brew unit, rinse it under lukewarm water without detergent, let it
  dry and push it back until it locks (E5 if it is not locked).
- Wash the water tank.

DESCALING: EVERY 3 MONTHS, OR WHEN THE DISPLAY SHOWS E3
With hard water (above 18 dH on the test strip), descale every 2 months.
Use Acme DS-100 descaler. One bottle (200 ml) is enough for two descalings.
Do not use vinegar: it damages the seals, and scale damage is not covered by the warranty.

K-200 (about 20 minutes)
1. Remove the water filter from the tank.
2. Pour 100 ml of DS-100 into the tank and add water up to 1 L.
3. Put a large bowl under the group head and the steam wand.
4. Hold the 1-cup and the steam button together for 3 seconds. The buttons blink.
5. The machine pumps the solution in steps. Wait until the buttons stop blinking.
6. Rinse the tank, fill it with fresh water up to MAX and press the 2-cup button
   three times to rinse.
7. Put the water filter back.

K-300 (about 25 minutes)
1. Choose Menu > Care > Descale.
2. Follow the display: remove the filter, add 100 ml of DS-100 and water up to the
   descaling mark, empty the drip tray when asked.
3. After the rinse cycle the display returns to the drinks menu.

CLEANING TABLET (K-300, ABOUT EVERY 200 DRINKS)
Choose Menu > Care > Clean, put one CL-TAB tablet in the brew unit compartment when the
display asks, and confirm.

MF-1
Never descale the MF-1 with DS-100. If milk has burnt on, fill the jug with warm water
and a drop of washing-up liquid, wait 10 minutes, then wash it with a soft sponge.
