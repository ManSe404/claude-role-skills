# Shipping policy

## Where we ship

Serbia and the EU member states. We do not ship outside the EU.

Orders for Serbia leave our warehouse in Novi Sad; orders for the EU leave our warehouse in
Bratislava, so there are no customs fees.

## How long

- Serbia: 2-4 working days
- EU: 4-8 working days

Orders placed before 13:00 on a working day leave the warehouse the same day.

## How much

- Serbia: free from 10,000 RSD, otherwise 450 RSD
- EU: free from 120 EUR, otherwise 9.90 EUR

## Tracking

When the parcel leaves the warehouse you get an e-mail with a tracking link. Your order status
is also in your account in the Acme shop, under Orders.

## Damaged parcels

If the parcel is visibly damaged, you may refuse it. If you find damage after opening it, report
it to support within 48 hours with photos of the box and the product.
