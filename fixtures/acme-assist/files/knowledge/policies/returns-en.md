# Returns policy (right of withdrawal)

Applies to products bought in the Acme online shop and delivered in Serbia or the EU.

## 14 days

You can withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days of delivery, without giving a reason.

## How to return a product

1. Contact support within the 14 days (chat or support@example.com) and give your order number.
2. Our support staff check the order and approve the return. Only staff can approve a return.
3. You receive an RMA number by e-mail. We cannot accept a parcel without an RMA number.
4. Send the product within 14 days of receiving the RMA number, complete with all accessories and
   if possible in the original packaging. Write the RMA number on the parcel.

## Refund

We refund the purchase price and the original standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving
the product, with the payment method you used. If the product shows use beyond what is needed to
try it, for example coffee residue or scale, we may deduct the loss in value.

## Return costs

You pay the return shipping, unless the product is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Faulty products

If a product is faulty, you do not need to withdraw from the purchase: see the warranty policy.

## Not returnable once opened

Descaler (DS-100), cleaning tablets (CL-TAB) and water filter cartridges, once the seal is broken.
