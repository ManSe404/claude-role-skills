# Warranty policy

Applies to the Kestrel K-200, the Kestrel K-300 and the MF-1 milk frother bought from Acme
Devices or an authorised reseller in Serbia or the EU.

Effective 1 October 2026. Replaces the version of 1 March 2025.

## How long

The warranty lasts 2 years from the date of purchase. A repaired or replaced product keeps the
warranty that is left on the original purchase.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship that appear in normal household use.

## What is not covered

- Damage from scale when the machine was not descaled as the manual says (every 3 months, or when
  the display shows E3)
- Wear parts: the group gasket, the brew unit seals, water filter cartridges and the MF-1 whisk
  spring
- Commercial use, for example in offices, cafes or rentals
- Damage from falls, liquid in the base, the wrong voltage, or repairs by anyone other than an
  authorised service
- Cosmetic wear such as scratches and discolouration

## Proof of purchase

The receipt or invoice with the date of purchase. Digital receipts and the order confirmation
e-mail from the Acme shop are accepted.

## How to make a claim

1. Contact support (chat or support@example.com) with the model, the serial number (on the label
   under the drip tray), the proof of purchase and a short description of the fault.
2. Support checks the case. If it is a warranty case, our staff issue an RMA number and a free
   courier pick-up.
3. Pack the product, if possible in the original box, write the RMA number on the box and give it
   to the courier. Parcels without an RMA number are refused.
4. We repair or replace the product within 30 days of receiving it.

## Dead on arrival

If a product does not work when you first unpack it, report it within 30 days of delivery. Once
support confirms the fault, our staff issue an RMA number and we send a new unit instead of
repairing the old one.

## Your statutory rights

This warranty does not limit your rights under the consumer protection law of Serbia or of your
EU country.
