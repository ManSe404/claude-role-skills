# Frequently asked questions

## Machines

**Which coffee should I use in the K-200?**
Any espresso grind. Pre-ground coffee works, but freshly ground beans give more crema. The
grind should be fine enough that a double shot takes 25-30 seconds.

**Can I use ground coffee in the K-300?**
No. The K-300 grinds whole beans and has no chute for ground coffee. Do not put ground,
caramelised or flavoured beans in the hopper; they block the grinder (error E6).

**My machine shows E3. What now?**
The machine needs descaling. Follow the descaling steps in the cleaning guide. It takes about
20 minutes on the K-200 and 25 minutes on the K-300.

**How often do I need to descale?**
Every 3 months, or when the display shows E3. With hard water, every 2 months.

**Which water should I use?**
Fresh cold tap water with the water filter in the tank. Do not use sparkling or distilled water.

**My K-300 shows E5.**
The brew unit is missing or not locked. Push it in until it clicks and close the side door.

**The MF-1 light blinks red.**
The frother was switched on empty or got too hot. Unplug it, let it cool for 10 minutes and try
again with milk.

## Orders and delivery

**How long does delivery take?**
2-4 working days in Serbia and 4-8 working days in the EU. See the shipping policy.

**Where can I see my order status?**
In your account in the Acme shop, under Orders. You also get a tracking link by e-mail when the
parcel leaves the warehouse.

**Do you ship outside the EU?**
No. We ship to Serbia and to EU member states only.

## Warranty and returns

**How long is the warranty?**
2 years from the date of purchase, for the K-200, the K-300 and the MF-1.

**Can I return a machine I bought online?**
Yes, within 14 days of delivery. Contact support first: you need an RMA number, and our staff
approve every return. Parcels without an RMA number are refused. See the returns policy.

**Is my warranty valid if I bought the machine in another EU country?**
Yes, if you bought it from Acme Devices or an authorised reseller in Serbia or the EU.
