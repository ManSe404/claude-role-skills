KESTREL ERROR CODES (K-200 AND K-300)
Version 2026-06

The display shows a code when the machine needs attention. E5 and E6 appear on the
K-300 only. The MF-1 milk frother has no display and no error codes.

E1  Water tank empty or not seated
    Models: K-200, K-300
    Fill the tank up to MAX and push it down until it clicks.

E2  No water flow (air in the system)
    Models: K-200, K-300
    K-200: run hot water through the steam wand for 30 seconds.
    K-300: choose Hot water and let it run for 30 seconds.
    If E2 comes back, descale the machine.

E3  Descaling needed
    Models: K-200, K-300
    Run the descaling programme (see the cleaning guide).

E4  Drip tray full or missing
    Models: K-200, K-300 (on the K-300 also the grounds container)
    Empty the drip tray (and the grounds container) and put it back.

E5  Brew unit missing or not locked
    Models: K-300
    Insert the brew unit until it locks and close the side door.

E6  Grinder blocked
    Models: K-300
    Switch off, empty the bean hopper and remove foreign objects.
    If E6 comes back, contact support.

E7  Overheated
    Models: K-200, K-300
    Switch off and let the machine cool for 30 minutes.

E8  Temperature sensor fault
    Models: K-200, K-300
    Switch off, unplug and contact support. Do not use the machine.

E9  Pump or electronics fault
    Models: K-200, K-300
    Switch off, unplug and contact support. Do not use the machine.

If a code comes back after you followed the steps, contact support with the model,
the serial number and the code. E8 and E9 are always a service case: do not open the
machine yourself.
