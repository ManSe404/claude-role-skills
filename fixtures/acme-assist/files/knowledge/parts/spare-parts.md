## Wear parts

The group gasket, the brew unit seals, water filter cartridges and the MF-1 whisk spring wear out
with use and are not covered by the warranty.

## Which part do I need?

- Water leaks around the portafilter (K-200): SP-GASKET-58.
- The water tastes of chlorine or the filter is older than 2 months: SP-FILTER-W.
- E5 keeps coming back although the brew unit is locked (K-300): contact support before you order
  SP-BREW-300.
