# Kestrel K-300 bean-to-cup machine: user manual

Model: K-300 (K300-BLK black, K300-SLV silver)
Manual version 2.4, July 2026

## In the box

- K-300 machine with a 1.8 L water tank and a 250 g bean hopper
- Milk tube set (SP-MILK-300) and a 0.5 L milk carafe
- Water filter cartridge (SP-FILTER-W)
- Two cleaning tablets (CL-TAB) and a water hardness test strip
- This manual and the quick-start sheet

## Before first use

1. Rinse the water tank, fill it with fresh cold water up to MAX and seat it firmly. If the tank
   is not seated, the display shows E1.
2. Fill the bean hopper with whole beans. Never put ground coffee, caramelised or flavoured beans
   in the hopper.
3. Switch on with the main switch at the back, then press the standby button on the front.
4. Choose the language and the water hardness (from the test strip) on the display.
5. The machine heats up and rinses the coffee outlet. Water runs into the drip tray; this is normal.

## Drinks

The display offers Espresso, Lungo, Americano, Cappuccino, Latte macchiato and Hot water.

- Strength: 1 to 5 beans on the display.
- Grind: turn the grind dial from 1 (fine) to 7 (coarse). Change the grind only while the grinder
  is running.
- Cup size: hold a drink button for 3 seconds to set its volume.

## Milk drinks

1. Fill the carafe with cold milk and put it in the fridge or next to the machine.
2. Connect the milk tube to the milk outlet and put the other end in the carafe.
3. Choose Cappuccino or Latte macchiato.
4. After each milk drink the display asks to rinse the milk system. Put a cup under the outlet and
   confirm.

## Brew unit

Take the brew unit out once a week: open the side door, press the red button, pull the unit out
and rinse it under lukewarm water without detergent. Let it dry and push it back until it locks.
If the brew unit is missing or not locked, the display shows E5.

## Care

- Empty the drip tray and the grounds container every day. When either is full or missing, the
  display shows E4.
- Descale every 3 months, or when the display shows E3 (Menu > Care > Descale).
- Run a cleaning tablet (CL-TAB) when the display asks, about every 200 drinks.
- Replace the water filter every 2 months.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|------------|
| E1 | Water tank empty or not seated | Fill the tank and seat it firmly |
| E2 | No water flow (air in the system) | Choose Hot water and let it run for 30 seconds |
| E3 | Descaling needed | Run the descaling programme |
| E4 | Drip tray or grounds container full or missing | Empty both and put them back |
| E5 | Brew unit missing or not locked | Insert the brew unit until it locks and close the door |
| E6 | Grinder blocked | Switch off, empty the hopper and remove foreign objects |
| E7 | Overheated | Switch off and let the machine cool for 30 minutes |
| E8 | Temperature sensor fault | Switch off, unplug and contact support |
| E9 | Pump or electronics fault | Switch off, unplug and contact support |

If E6 comes back after you emptied the hopper, contact support.

## Technical data

- Power: 1450 W, 220-240 V, 50 Hz
- Pump pressure: 15 bar
- Water tank: 1.8 L; bean hopper: 250 g; grounds container: 12 portions
- Weight: 8.9 kg
