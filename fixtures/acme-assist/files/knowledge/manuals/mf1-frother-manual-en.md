# MF-1 milk frother: user manual

Model: MF-1 (MF1-WHT white, MF1-BLK black)
Manual version 1.2, March 2026

## Parts

- Jug with a non-stick inside and a lid
- Base with power cable
- Whisk with spring for foam (SP-WHISK-MF1), stored in the lid
- Flat whisk for warm milk

## Using the frother

1. Put the whisk on the axle in the jug.
2. Pour in cold milk: up to the lower mark (150 ml) for foam, up to the upper mark (300 ml) for
   warm milk.
3. Put the jug on the base and close the lid.
4. Press the button:
   - short press: warm foam (red light)
   - hold for 2 seconds: warm milk (red light)
   - press twice: cold foam (blue light)
5. The frother stops on its own after 2-3 minutes and the light goes off.

## Tips

- Use milk straight from the fridge. Whole milk gives the firmest foam.
- Plant drinks marked "barista" foam best.
- Never fill above the marks; the milk rises while it foams.

## Cleaning

Unplug the base and let the jug cool. Wash the inside with a soft sponge and warm water. The lid
and the whisks can go in the dishwasher; the jug and the base cannot. Never put the jug or the
base under running water or in water.

## Problems

- The red light blinks: the jug was switched on empty or got too hot. Unplug, let it cool for
  10 minutes and add milk.
- No foam: check that the whisk with the spring sits on the axle, and use colder milk.
- Milk burns on the bottom: clean the jug after every use.

The MF-1 has no display and no error codes.

## Technical data

- Power: 500 W, 220-240 V, 50 Hz
- Capacity: 300 ml warm milk, 150 ml foam

## Warranty

The MF-1 has a 2-year warranty from the date of purchase. The whisk spring is a wear part.
