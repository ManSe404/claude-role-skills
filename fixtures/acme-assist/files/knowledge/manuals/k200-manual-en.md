# Kestrel K-200 espresso machine: user manual

Model: K-200 (K200-BLK black, K200-WHT white)
Manual version 3.1, June 2026

## In the box

- K-200 machine with a 1.2 L removable water tank
- Portafilter with a single and a double basket
- Tamper and measuring spoon
- Water filter cartridge (SP-FILTER-W)
- This manual and the quick-start sheet

## Before first use

1. Remove all packaging. Rinse the water tank, the portafilter and the baskets.
2. Soak the water filter cartridge for 5 minutes, put it in the tank and fill the tank with fresh
   cold water up to MAX.
3. Push the tank down until it clicks. If the tank is not seated, the display shows E1.
4. Plug in the machine and press the power button. The buttons blink while the machine heats up
   (about 40 seconds).
5. Without coffee, put a cup under the group head and press the 2-cup button twice to flush the
   system.

## Making espresso

1. Fill the single basket with 7-9 g or the double basket with 14-18 g of ground coffee.
2. Tamp evenly and lock the portafilter into the group head by turning it to the right.
3. Press 1-cup (30 ml) or 2-cup (60 ml). The pump works at 15 bar.
4. A good shot takes 25-30 seconds. If it runs much faster, grind finer; if it only drips, grind
   coarser.

To change a cup volume, hold the 1-cup or 2-cup button until the cup holds as much as you want,
then let go. The machine remembers the new volume.

## Steaming milk

1. Press the steam button. It blinks while the machine heats up, then stays lit.
2. Purge the steam wand into the drip tray for 2 seconds.
3. Hold the wand just under the milk surface, open the steam knob and lower the jug as the milk
   expands.
4. Close the knob and wipe the wand with a damp cloth at once.

For milk foam without the wand, use the MF-1 milk frother.

## Care

- Empty and rinse the drip tray every day. When it is full, the float rises and the display
  shows E4.
- Clean the portafilter and the baskets after each use.
- Replace the water filter every 2 months.
- Descale every 3 months, or when the display shows E3. See the cleaning guide.
- Replace the group gasket (SP-GASKET-58) once a year, or when water leaks around the portafilter.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|------------|
| E1 | Water tank empty or not seated | Fill the tank and push it down until it clicks |
| E2 | No water flow (air in the system) | Run hot water through the steam wand for 30 seconds |
| E3 | Descaling needed | Run the descaling programme |
| E4 | Drip tray full or missing | Empty the drip tray and put it back |
| E7 | Overheated | Switch off and let the machine cool for 30 minutes |
| E8 | Temperature sensor fault | Switch off, unplug and contact support |
| E9 | Pump or electronics fault | Switch off, unplug and contact support |

E5 and E6 appear on the K-300 only. The full list is in the error code list.

## Technical data

- Pump pressure: 15 bar
- Water tank: 1.2 L
- Weight: 4.1 kg
