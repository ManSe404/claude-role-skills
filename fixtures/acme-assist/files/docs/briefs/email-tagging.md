# Brief: tagging the shared inbox

From: Jelena Radin, Head of Customer Service, Acme Devices (jelena.radin@example.com)
Date: 21 October 2026

This is a small request, separate from support assistant v2.

## Today

About 200 e-mails per day arrive at our shared inbox, support@example.com, in Serbian and
English. One of the support agents sorts them by hand every morning and again after lunch, so an
e-mail can wait half a day before the right person sees it.

## What we want

- Sort each e-mail into one of 6 categories: order, technical, returns, invoice, partnership,
  spam.
- The tag goes to our helpdesk, HelpLine, through its tag API. Agents work their queues by tag.
- No reply is drafted and nothing is sent to customers.
- Tagging within 5 minutes of arrival is fine.

## How good it has to be

Mistakes are cheap: an agent re-tags an e-mail in seconds. About 5% wrong tags is acceptable.

## Limits

- Budget: about €50 per month.
- The e-mails contain personal data (names, addresses, phone numbers, order numbers). The same
  rule as for support v2 applies: customer data stays in the EU.
