# Brief: reporting for Acme Bakery

From: Finance, Acme Bakery
Date: 6 October 2026

Acme Bakery is a sister company of Acme Foods. We bake for our own shops and want one place for sales against budget
instead of the copies of the same export that go around by e-mail today.

## What we have

- One ERP with orders, invoices, customers and products.
- One budget spreadsheet that finance updates every month.
- Nothing else is planned.

Together this is 3 GB in total, growing about 40 MB a month.

## What we need

- Sales against budget by shop and product group, joined across the ERP and the spreadsheet.
- The figures refreshed once a day, overnight. Nobody needs them fresher than that.

## Who will use it

- 10 people read the reports in Power BI.
- We have one analyst, who knows SQL and looks after the Power BI reports.
- We have no data engineer and no plan to hire one.
